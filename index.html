What is Cadence

Payroll that pays as work happens, not two weeks later.

Cadence is a payroll dashboard for businesses. You give each person a weekly amount and a pay rhythm; Cadence splits that total into small RLUSD payments and sends them on schedule over the XRP Ledger. You fund the stream — Cadence keeps the time. Every payment settles wallet to wallet in seconds and lands on-chain, where your team can export real payment history as proof of income.

Who it's for

Built for the people who pay — and the people who get paid.

For businesses

  • Pay contractors and distributed teams without running a payroll cycle per region.
  • Set a pay rhythm for each person — their weekly total, divided into payments that land while work happens.
  • No float and no custodian: payments settle wallet to wallet in seconds.

For workers

  • Stop waiting on the two-week cycle — pay arrives the moment work happens.
  • Money lands in the wallet you control, not a company account.
  • Every payment is on-chain and exportable as proof of income from the Cadence app.
